00 / Premise

Personal multi-agent systems require repeated allocation decisions.

Users first need a working configuration. During use, they decide which workflow stages to delegate, which outputs to monitor, and which decisions to retain.

Central question

How does the boundary of delegation move across a workflow and over time?

01 / Conceptual model

Deploy. Delegate. Accumulate.

The DDA model describes personal-agent use as a recurrent process comprising deployment, stage-level delegation, and memory accumulation.

Delegation varied
by stage and experience.

Delegation varied across workflow stages. The longitudinal analysis also records within-stage reductions after unsuccessful outcomes.

01 Deployment feasibility

Four of five users completed the guided deployment.

Completed attempts clustered near the intended 30-minute target. Completers reported a mean SUS of 72.25, while the mean across all five attempts was 66.2.

04 / Research integrity

Study design defines
the scope of inference.

Every result on this page is paired with the inference boundary that governs its interpretation.

01

Causal inference

Survey, event, and memory results are observational. Temporal order and correlation do not identify a mechanism.

02

Equivalence testing

A non-significant quality difference does not establish equivalence or non-inferiority.

03

External validity

Small, single-institution convenience samples constrain precision and generalizability.

04

Participant data

Original consent prohibits public release of row-level responses, dated events, and linked records.

Public evidence

Public materials and restricted participant records.

The repository publishes instruments, codebooks, analysis code, aggregate tables, and all 33 research figures. Restricted inputs are represented by documented schemas and cryptographic hashes.

05 / Research artifact

The deployment protocol standardizes setup and records its outcomes.

The Self-Bootstrapping Deployment Protocol encodes setup knowledge in a guided document: gathering configuration, creating a collaboration structure, initializing memory, and conducting a bootstrap interview.
