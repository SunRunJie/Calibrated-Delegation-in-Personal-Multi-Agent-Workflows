# Informed consent form

**Study title:** Calibrated Delegation in Personal Multi-Agent Workflows: Deployment, Task Boundaries, and Continued Use

**Researcher:** Runjie Sun, School of Information Management, Nanjing University

**Purpose:** This study examines how users of a personal AI chief-of-staff system delegate tasks, adjust delegation boundaries, and continue or discontinue use in real workflows. The findings will inform the design and evaluation of personal multi-agent systems.

## 1. What participation may involve

You may be invited to take part in one or more of the following activities. You may consent to each activity separately.

1. **Survey:** an anonymous questionnaire of approximately 28 items, taking about eight minutes.
2. **Interview:** a 20-30 minute audio-recorded interview about your experience. The recording will be used only to prepare and analyze a transcript.
3. **Log export:** permission for the researcher to review and analyze memory files, `timeline.md`, and selected conversations from your personal AI chief-of-staff system for behavioral coding.
4. **Deployment usability test:** an independent deployment attempt using only the installation guide while a researcher records the process without intervening.
5. **Comparative experiment:** completion of standardized tasks under single-agent and multi-agent configurations, taking approximately one hour.

## 2. Voluntary participation and withdrawal

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time, for any reason, without explanation. Withdrawal will not affect your relationship with the researcher or institution.

## 3. Privacy and data protection

1. The survey is anonymous. If survey responses are linked to logs or memory indicators, use a self-generated random link code. The code should not reveal your identity.
2. Interview recordings and transcripts will be accessible only to the researcher. Audio recordings will be destroyed after transcription and analysis.
3. Data will be anonymized and de-identified. Results will be presented only for research purposes in aggregate form without directly identifying information.
4. Before exporting logs or conversations, remove confidential institutional information and personal information about third parties.
5. Participant-level data will not be distributed externally. Research reports will present anonymized aggregate results.

## 4. Research integrity

The study follows Nanjing University's research-integrity guidance. The researcher remains responsible for the study design, data provenance, analysis decisions, interpretation, and conclusions. No data will be generated or fabricated to replace participant observations.
